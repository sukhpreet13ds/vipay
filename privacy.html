1. Introduction

Welcome to VPAY Advisory (OPC) Private Limited ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect the privacy of our clients, visitors, and users. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard personal and business information when you visit our website or engage our professional tax consultancy, accounting, and statutory advisory services.

2. Information We Collect

In the course of providing tax consultation and statutory compliance services, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact Information: Name, phone / WhatsApp numbers, email addresses, and postal addresses.
  • Statutory & Identification Data: PAN, GSTIN, TAN, Aadhaar (where statutorily needed), business incorporation numbers, and relevant government IDs required for filings.
  • Financial & Accounting Records: Invoices, balance sheets, bank account details, ledger reconciliations, payroll data, and transaction summaries necessary for ITR, GST, and audit preparation.
  • Communication Details: Records of enquiries submitted via our website form, WhatsApp chats, email correspondence, or telephonic discussions.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely for legitimate professional and statutory purposes, including:

  • Preparing and filing GST, Income Tax, TDS, and other statutory returns.
  • Handling tax assessment proceedings, scrutiny replies, departmental notices, and appellate representation.
  • Providing accounting, bookkeeping, financial reporting, and entity registration assistance.
  • Communicating compliance deadlines, tax updates, and responding to client enquiries.
  • Complying with statutory, legal, and regulatory obligations under Indian law.

4. Confidentiality & Data Protection

Tax and financial documents contain sensitive business information. We maintain strict confidentiality standards and implement appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to prevent unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your information. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial records to third parties.

5. Third-Party Disclosures

Your information is only shared when strictly necessary for the performance of our professional engagements:

  • With government authorities (e.g., GST Portal, Income Tax Department, MCA, TRACES) for statutory filing and representation.
  • With professional associates (auditors, company secretaries, or legal counsels) working directly on your mandate with your consent.
  • When required by law, court order, or governmental regulation.

6. Data Retention

We retain professional records and communication data for as long as required to fulfill the advisory engagement, comply with statutory tax retention periods under Indian taxation laws, or resolve potential legal/statutory queries.

7. Your Rights

Clients have the right to review, update, or request corrections to their personal and contact details held by us. If you wish to inspect or modify your records, please contact our office directly.
